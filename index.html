1. Introduction

Welcome to Gobblet Gobblers 3D ("the Game"), available on iOS (App Store) and Android (Google Play). The Game is developed by Mahmoud Khairy, an independent developer based in Egypt, who is responsible for the information described in this policy. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we collect, use, and share your information.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

Data Purpose
Display Name & Avatar Shown to other players in matches, friends lists and leaderboards
Chat Messages (match chat and direct messages) In-game communication. An automatic word filter runs on your device.
Reports & Blocks Community safety. A report stores who reported whom, the reason and a copy of the reported content (for a direct-message report, up to the last 20 messages of that conversation from the last 30 days) so it can be reviewed.
Support Emails Answering you. The pre-filled email from Settings → Contact Support includes the app version and your player ID; you can edit it before sending.

2.2 Account & Sign-In

  • Guest: an anonymous account ID is created so you can play online. No name or email.
  • Sign in with Apple (iOS): we receive a stable Apple user ID and the email address Apple shares with us, which may be a private relay address. Apple may also share your name; we do not store it. We keep an Apple-issued token only so the Game's access to your Apple ID can be revoked when you delete your account.
  • Google Sign-In (Android): we receive your Google account ID and email address, which we use to identify your account and keep your progress across devices. We do not use your Google name, and we do not receive your password or your contacts.

2.3 Automatic Collection

Data Purpose
Game Statistics & Match Results Leaderboards, matchmaking and daily tasks
Coins, Items & Wallet Transactions Keeping your balance and inventory correct
Purchase Records (store transaction ID, product, platform, date and status) Delivering and restoring purchases and handling refunds. Payments are processed by Apple or Google; we never see your card details.
Approximate Country Looked up from your IP address through ipinfo.io (or ipapi.co as a fallback). We store only the two-letter country code, shown as a flag on the leaderboard. We do not store your IP address.
Push Notification Token Notifying you of new messages
Usage Analytics (Google Firebase Analytics) Understanding how the Game is used and how ads perform: app and screen events, ad events, device model, OS version and an app-instance identifier. Not linked to your account ID.
Advertising Identifiers (Apple IDFA with your permission; Android Advertising ID) Ads served and measured by Google AdMob (see section 4)
Crash & Error Logs Diagnosing crashes and fixing bugs

Crash and error reports describe the technical failure: the error message, the stack trace, the app version, the device's operating system and browser-engine version, and which screen was open. They are automatically scrubbed of email addresses, account identifiers and access tokens before they leave your device, they do not include your name, your account ID or a device ID, and our crash reporting provider is instructed not to store IP addresses.

Voice chat (Squad Party): live audio between squad members, only after you tap the microphone button. It is never recorded or stored. Voice travels directly between devices (peer-to-peer), so squad members' devices can see each other's IP address; when a direct connection is not possible it is relayed through Cloudflare. Local (LAN) play finds games on your own Wi-Fi network and stays on that network.

3. How We Use Information

  • Enable online multiplayer, matchmaking, friends, chat and voice
  • Process in-app purchases and virtual currency
  • Display ads via Google AdMob, including rewarded ads you choose to watch
  • Track daily tasks and rewards
  • Prevent cheating, fraud and abuse, and review reports
  • Detect, diagnose and fix crashes and errors
  • Improve game performance and features

If you are in the EEA or UK, we rely on: performance of our contract with you (providing the Game); legitimate interests (security, anti-abuse, analytics and fixing crashes); your consent (personalized ads); and legal obligations (purchase and tax records).

4. Advertising, Consent & Tracking

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: Google's consent form asks for your choice, and ads stay non-personalized until you consent. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy Options.
  • iPhone: Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt asks for your permission before the advertising identifier can be used. If you decline, Apple does not share it.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings.

6. Notifications

A new-message notification shows the sender's display name and a short preview of the message (up to 80 characters, word-filtered). To deliver it, the preview passes through Cloudflare, Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and, on iPhone, Apple Push Notification service. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

8. Your Rights

Delete Account: Settings → Profile → Delete Account, or email us.

Personalized Ads: change your choice in Settings → Privacy Options (EEA/UK), decline tracking on iPhone, or reset/delete your Advertising ID on Android.

Notifications: turn them off in your device settings.

Access Your Data: Contact us to request a copy of your data.

9. Children's Privacy

Gobblet Gobblers 3D is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us immediately.

10. Data Security

We protect your data using:

  • HTTPS/TLS encryption for all data transmission
  • Secure sign-in with Apple and Google
  • Row-level security in our database, so players can only reach their own data
  • Server-side checks for coins, rewards and purchases, which are verified with Apple and Google
  • Regular security audits

11. International Transfers

Your data may be processed in the European Union (Supabase - Ireland; Sentry - Germany) and in other countries including the United States (Google, Apple, Cloudflare). We rely on our providers' standard contractual safeguards for international transfers.
